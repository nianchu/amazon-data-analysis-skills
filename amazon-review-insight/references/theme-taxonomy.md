# Theme Taxonomy

Use hierarchical codes and retain a free-text detail field.

1. Signal and compatibility
   - HDMI handshake, HDCP, EDID, resolution/refresh, HDR/Dolby Vision, ARC/eARC, CEC, audio format, source/display/audio-device compatibility.
2. Audio outcome
   - no audio, dropouts, noise, latency, channel limitation, downmixing, volume control, format passthrough.
3. Video outcome
   - black screen, flicker, resolution loss, refresh loss, HDR/color issue, wake/sleep instability.
4. Setup and usability
   - wiring confusion, switch labels, manual quality, power behavior, firmware, remote, plug-and-play expectation.
5. Build and reliability
   - heat, connector looseness, power failure, intermittent operation, early failure, long-term durability.
6. Value and expectation
   - price-value, misleading claim, missing accessory, warranty, support, packaging, delivery damage.
7. Positive outcomes
   - solved legacy-equipment connection, console/PC use, TV apps/eARC use, home theater, streaming, capture/monitoring, easy setup, stable high-bandwidth passthrough.

For each theme also code: scenario, source device, display, audio device, connection path, desired outcome, failure point, severity, fixability, and evidence confidence.
